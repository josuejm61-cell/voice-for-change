Habitat for Humanity Laredo

Habitat for Humanity helps families and communities by working toward safe and affordable housing. Having a stable place to live can make a major difference in a person's quality of life.

People can support Habitat for Humanity through volunteering, donating, helping with projects, or learning more about housing needs in their community.

Feed the Hungry

Feed the Hungry works to help people who may not have reliable access to food. Hunger can affect individuals and families in many communities, which makes programs that provide meals and food assistance especially important.

People can help by volunteering, donating food or money, and spreading awareness about programs that help those experiencing food insecurity.

South Texas Food Bank

The South Texas Food Bank works to fight hunger by providing food and other assistance to individuals and families in need. Organizations like this are important because they help make sure people in the community have access to basic necessities.

People can support the food bank by donating food, volunteering, giving financial support, or helping spread awareness about hunger in the community.

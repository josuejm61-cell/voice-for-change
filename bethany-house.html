Bethany House of Laredo

Bethany House of Laredo provides assistance to people who are experiencing difficult situations. Organizations like Bethany House can provide important services and support to people who need help getting back on their feet.

Community members can make a difference by volunteering, donating needed items, supporting programs, and helping others learn about available resources.

# Voice for Change Project Proposal

## 1. Project Description

**Voice for Change** is a website that focuses on raising awareness of the importance of encouraging positive, inspiring change. The primary objective is to make a positive difference in our communities. When visiting the site, we can see various organizations and their functions. My goal is to educate people about volunteering, donating, sharing information, or getting involved. 

The project is important to me because many people want to help with issues that we can all relate to, and some of us don’t even know where to start. This website will provide visitors with a place to learn about causes and take action to make a difference. 

This website will be targeted at teenagers, young adults, families, and all community members. Plus, it will be for all people who care about others when others are in the most need. They can gather ideas and resources to get started. 

## 2. Feature Plan

### Project 01 - Week 05 Prototype

- **Homepage** - for the homepage, I will set up some main buttons and clickable sections for navigation.
- **Causes Section:** - In the causes section, I will add different types of causes that are around in my local community. 
- **About/Mission Section:**- In the Mission Section, I will add the reason for my page, what my goal is, and what it is that we should strive for. 
- **Ways to Help:** - Different key points on how we can help and where to contribute. 
**Navigation Menu:** - The navigation menu will make the page more friendly to clicks and navigation buttons. 

### Project 02 - Week 08 Final MVP

- **Individual Cause Pages:** On the pages, I will try to include as much detail as possible on all causes and information about them as well. 
- **Improved Visual Design:** The site will include a more polished layout, images, icons, and consistent colors.
- **Get Involved Section:** I will include an option where visitors will be able to find different options for acting based on the type of help they are interested in.
- **Accessibility Improvements:** Overall, I will polish the site for a better-looking page that is friendly to users and navigation-friendly.
